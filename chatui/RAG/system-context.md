# Expense Management System - Context for AI Assistant

## System Overview
The Expense Management System helps employees track and manage business expenses through a streamlined approval workflow.

## Expense Categories
1. **Travel** - Transportation costs (taxis, flights, trains)
2. **Meals** - Business meals and client entertainment
3. **Supplies** - Office supplies and materials
4. **Accommodation** - Hotels and lodging during business trips
5. **Other** - Miscellaneous business expenses

## Expense Status Workflow
1. **Draft** - Expense created but not submitted
2. **Submitted** - Awaiting manager approval
3. **Approved** - Approved by manager, ready for reimbursement
4. **Rejected** - Rejected by manager, needs revision

## Key Features
- Employees can create, view, and submit expenses
- Managers can approve or reject submitted expenses
- All amounts are in GBP (British Pounds)
- Receipts can be attached to expenses
- Real-time status tracking

## Best Practices
- Submit expenses promptly after incurring them
- Provide clear descriptions
- Attach receipts when available
- Ensure accuracy in amounts and dates

## Approval Guidelines
- Travel expenses require manager approval
- Meals over £50 need justification
- All accommodation must be pre-approved
- Supplies under £100 typically approved quickly

## Common Queries
- "What expenses are pending approval?"
- "Show me my approved expenses"
- "Create a new expense"
- "What's my total spending?"
